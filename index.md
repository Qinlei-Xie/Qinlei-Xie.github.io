## 🔬 Research Interests

My research focuses on building spatial world models for AI, enabling them to learn and reason from spatial information just like humans do.

Meanwhile, I am interested in memory reliability — the human brain should not produce confused hallucinations, and neither should AI's memory systems.

**Keywords:** Spatial reasoning, Spatial memory, World models, Embodied AI, Agents

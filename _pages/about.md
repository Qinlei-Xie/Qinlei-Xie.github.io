About Me
Hello! 👋 I am Qinlei Xie (谢钦磊), an undergraduate student (Class of 2024) at Dalian University of Technology, majoring in Big Data Management and Applications. I am seeking PhD positions for Fall 2028.
I am currently a research intern at Prof. Yiming Li's lab (incoming Assistant Professor at Tsinghua University), and I am also conducting research training with Prof. Weiwen Liu at the APEX Lab, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
(By the way, both professors are incredibly supportive mentors. If you are interested in our research directions, feel free to contact them via email!)

Research Interests
My research interests primarily focus on LLM-empowered Spatial Reasoning and Memory. As I progress to my junior year, I plan to explore related areas such as Multi-Robot Systems and 3D Reconstruction.

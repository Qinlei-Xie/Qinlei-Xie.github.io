// Research Interests

Research

My research focuses on building spatial world models for AI, enabling them to learn and reason from spatial information just like humans do. Meanwhile, I am interested in memory reliability — the human brain should not produce confused hallucinations, and neither should AI's memory systems.

🌐
Spatial Reasoning
How can AI understand and navigate 3D environments, infer spatial relationships, and build coherent maps of its surroundings?
🧠
World Models
Building internal simulations of the world that allow AI to predict, plan, and imagine unseen scenarios from limited observations.
🤖
Embodied AI
Grounding intelligence in physical or simulated agents that interact with and learn from the real world through action.
💾
Memory Systems
Designing reliable memory architectures for AI that avoid hallucinations and maintain consistent, retrievable knowledge over time.

Research Interests Humans understand space, store memories, and make decisions through the brain. I've been thinking: Does embodied AI also need such a "brain"? My research focuses on building spatial world models for AI, enabling them to learn and reason from spatial information just like humans do. Meanwhile, I am interested in memory reliability — the human brain should not produce confused hallucinations, and neither should AI's memory systems.
